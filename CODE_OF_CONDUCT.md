# Community Code of Conduct

Runtime Assurance Lab welcomes contributors regardless of background, geography or experience. We aim for **precise, respectful, evidence-based engineering discussion**.

## Expected behavior

- Critique code, claims, experimental methods and security assumptions—not the people who authored them.
- Cite evidence, acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes publicly when safe to do so.
- Welcome genuine questions and contributions from new maintainers.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property and the permissions of upstream projects.
- Preserve honest negative results and avoid exaggerated claims of security or production readiness.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hateful language, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, deliberate misinformation about tests, doxxing, or publication of another person's credentials, private infrastructure data or vulnerabilities without coordination.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove disruptive content and restrict participation to keep the project safe and useful. For sensitive security reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting public exploit details. For community concerns, contact maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub channel when available; do not put sensitive personal information in public issues.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions.
