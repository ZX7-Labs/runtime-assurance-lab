# Support and research questions

Runtime Assurance Lab is an **experimental, CPU-only research repository**, not a deployed product. There are no supported releases, customer support contracts, SLAs, authorized live Kubernetes collectors or guarantees about real GPU/model execution.

- **Getting started or reproducibility:** use the [cross-platform guide](docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md). For a public **sanitized** reproducible problem, choose a [GitHub issue form](https://github.com/ZX7-Labs/runtime-assurance-lab/issues/new/choose).
- **Research ideas, vendor comparisons and contribution tasks:** start at [contributor paths](docs/CONTRIBUTOR_PATHS.md), [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), then an appropriate open issue.
- **Vulnerabilities or sensitive findings:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do **not** put exploits, secrets, internal endpoints or real customer data in a public issue. Availability of private vulnerability reporting must be confirmed in repository settings.
- **Real-world study interest:** see [issue #4](https://github.com/ZX7-Labs/runtime-assurance-lab/issues/4); **do not upload real AICR/AIBOM exports**. This is not a verified confidential intake process.

Maintainers respond on a best-effort basis only. A response, fix, merge, community moderation capacity or product release is **not** guaranteed. Independent negative results and proposals to contribute upstream are welcome.
